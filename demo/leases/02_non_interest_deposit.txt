AGREEMENT OF LEASE: RESIDENTIAL PREMISES

PARTIES

This agreement is made between Coastal Lettings CC, Registration Number 2015/123456/23, a close corporation letting property in the ordinary course of its business, with its principal place of business at 210 Florida Road, Morningside, Durban, 4001 ("the Landlord") and Naledi Khumalo, Identity Number 7603055017085, of 33 Innes Road, Morningside, Durban, 4001, telephone 031 303 2211 ("the Tenant").

PREMISES

The Landlord lets to the Tenant the flat situated at Flat 4B, Sea Breeze Court, 88 Snell Parade, North Beach, Durban, 4001, which is let furnished as per the attached inventory.

DURATION

The lease is for a fixed term of twelve months. It commences on 01/04/2026 and ends on 31/03/2027.

RENTAL AND ESCALATION

The Tenant shall pay rental of R9 200 per month, in advance, on the first day of every month, by electronic funds transfer into the Landlord's account: Standard Bank, Account Number 10098765432, Branch 051001. The rental shall increase by 7,5% on the anniversary of the commencement date in each year.

DEPOSIT

The Tenant shall pay a deposit of R18 400 before occupation. The deposit will be held in the Landlord's current account and will not earn interest. The deposit, less any amounts owing, will be refunded to the Tenant within 30 days after the Tenant vacates.

TERMINATION

The Tenant may cancel this lease on twenty business days' written notice to the Landlord, subject to a reasonable cancellation penalty in terms of section 14 of the Consumer Protection Act. The Landlord will notify the Tenant of the expiry of the lease between 40 and 80 business days before the end date. If the Tenant does not elect otherwise the lease will continue month to month after the end date.

ACCESS

The Landlord may enter the premises on 24 hours' written notice to view their condition or to carry out repairs.

MAINTENANCE

The Landlord shall maintain the structure, roof, plumbing and electrical installation and keep the premises in a habitable condition. The Tenant shall keep the interior clean, replace light bulbs and report any defect to the Landlord in writing without delay.

BREACH

If rental is not paid within seven days of the due date, a late payment fee of R250 shall be payable in addition to interest at the prime rate plus 2% on the overdue amount.

GENERAL

This lease and the attached inventory record the whole agreement. Personal information supplied by the Tenant will be processed only for administering this lease and for credit screening, as required by the Protection of Personal Information Act.

Signed at Durban on 15 March 2026 for the Landlord by its duly authorised member and by the Tenant.
