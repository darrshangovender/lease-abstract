AGREEMENT OF LEASE

MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT made and entered into by and between

SANDTON PROPERTY HOLDINGS (PTY) LTD (Registration No 2012/034567/07), a company incorporated in the Republic of South Africa with its registered office at 4th Floor, 100 Grayston Drive, Sandton, 2196 ("the Landlord")

and

MBALI NDLOVU (Identity Number 9504305081087), of 12 Eighth Avenue, Melville, Johannesburg, 2092 ("the Tenant").

1. INTERPRETATION
1.1 In this lease "the Premises" means Unit 804, The Grayston, 120 Grayston Drive, Sandton, 2196, including one basement parking bay.

2. LEASE PERIOD
2.1 The Landlord lets the Premises to the Tenant for a fixed term of 24 (twenty-four) months commencing on 1 September 2026 and expiring on 31 August 2028.
2.2 This lease shall expire automatically on the expiry date and shall not be renewed or extended on a month-to-month basis. The Tenant shall vacate the Premises on the expiry date.
2.3 The Tenant has no right to cancel this lease before the expiry date. Should the Tenant vacate early, the Tenant remains liable for the rental for the remainder of the term.

3. RENTAL
3.1 The monthly rental is ZAR 15,500.00, payable in advance on the first day of each month by debit order.
3.2 The rental shall escalate by 7% (seven percent) on each anniversary of the commencement date.
3.3 All payments shall be made to Sandton Property Holdings (Pty) Ltd, First National Bank, account number 62987654321, branch code 250655.

4. DEPOSIT
4.1 The Tenant shall pay a deposit equal to two (2) months' rental, being ZAR 31,000.00, on or before the commencement date.
4.2 The deposit shall be invested in an interest-bearing account at not less than the savings rate and the interest shall accrue to the Tenant. Written proof of interest shall be furnished on request.
4.3 The parties shall conduct a joint inspection of the Premises at the commencement and at the termination of this lease.
4.4 The deposit and accrued interest shall be refunded within 7 (seven) days of termination, less the cost of repairing damage recorded at the outgoing inspection.

5. ACCESS
5.1 The Landlord or its agent may enter the Premises on 24 hours' written notice to inspect, repair or show the Premises to prospective tenants.

6. MAINTENANCE
6.1 The Landlord shall maintain:
(a) the structure, roof and exterior;
(b) the geyser, plumbing and electrical installations;
(c) the common property and lifts.
6.2 The Tenant shall:
(a) keep the interior clean and undamaged;
(b) replace light bulbs, fuses and tap washers;
(c) report any defect to the Landlord within 48 hours.

7. BREACH
7.1 Should the Tenant fail to pay rental on due date, interest shall accrue at 2% per month and an administration fee of ZAR 350.00 shall be charged.
7.2 If the Tenant remains in breach 7 (seven) days after written demand, the Landlord may cancel this lease and reclaim possession.

8. SPECIAL CONDITIONS
8.1 No subletting or short-term holiday letting of the Premises is permitted.

9. GENERAL
9.1 The Tenant consents to the processing of the personal information in this lease for the purposes of administering the lease, credit vetting and debt recovery, in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013.
9.2 Address for notices: the Tenant at the Premises; the Landlord at its registered office.

Signed at Sandton on 20 August 2026.
