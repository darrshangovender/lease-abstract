1.2 Sipho Dlamini, Identity Number 9109235112083, cell 082 555 0147, email sipho.dlamini@example.co.za ("the Tenant").

2. PREMISES

2.1 The Landlord lets to the Tenant the residential premises known as Unit 12, Umhlanga Sands, 14 Lagoon Drive, Umhlanga Rocks, 4320 ("the Premises"), together with one undercover parking bay. The Premises are let unfurnished.

3. DURATION

3.1 This lease is for a fixed term of 12 (twelve) months commencing on 1 March 2026 and terminating on 28 February 2027.

3.2 On expiry the lease shall continue on a month-to-month basis on the same terms unless the Tenant elects otherwise or the parties agree a new fixed term in writing.

4. RENTAL

4.1 The monthly rental is R 8 500,00 (eight thousand five hundred rand), payable monthly in advance on or before the 1st day of each month by debit order into the Landlord's nominated bank account.

4.2 Rental shall escalate by 6% (six percent) on each anniversary of the commencement date.

5. DEPOSIT

5.1 The Tenant shall pay a deposit of R 17 000,00 (seventeen thousand rand), equal to two (2) months' rental, on or before signature of this lease.

5.2 The Landlord shall invest the deposit in an interest-bearing account with a registered financial institution at a rate not less than the rate applicable to a savings account, and the interest shall accrue to the Tenant. The Landlord shall provide written proof of the interest earned on request.

5.3 The Landlord and the Tenant shall jointly inspect the Premises before the Tenant takes occupation and again within three days before the lease ends, and shall record any defects in a written list signed by both.

5.4 The deposit and interest, less the reasonable cost of repairing any damage caused by the Tenant, shall be refunded to the Tenant within 7 (seven) days of the expiry of this lease where no deductions are made, or within 14 (fourteen) days together with an itemised statement where deductions are made.

6. TERMINATION AND NOTICE

6.1 The Tenant may cancel this lease before the termination date by giving the Landlord 20 (twenty) business days' written notice, subject to a reasonable cancellation penalty as contemplated in section 14(3) of the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008.

6.2 The Landlord may cancel this lease on 20 (twenty) business days' written notice if the Tenant is in material breach and fails to cure the breach within 7 (seven) days of being called upon in writing to do so.

6.3 The Landlord shall notify the Tenant in writing of the expiry of this lease, and of any changes proposed, not more than 80 (eighty) and not less than 40 (forty) business days before the termination date.

7. ACCESS AND INSPECTION

7.1 The Landlord may enter the Premises to inspect them or to carry out repairs on not less than 24 (twenty-four) hours' written notice to the Tenant, at a reasonable time, except in an emergency.

8. MAINTENANCE

8.1 The Landlord is responsible for:
(a) structural repairs to the roof, walls and foundations;
(b) the plumbing, electrical installation and geyser, except where damage is caused by the Tenant;
(c) maintaining the exterior of the building and the common property.

8.2 The Tenant is responsible for:
(a) keeping the interior of the Premises clean and in good order;
(b) replacing light bulbs, tap washers and broken window panes;
(c) maintaining the garden and any pool.

9. BREACH AND LATE PAYMENT

9.1 Rental not paid by the 7th day of the month shall bear interest at 2% (two percent) per month from the due date until paid.

9.2 Should either party breach this lease and fail to cure the breach within 14 (fourteen) days of written demand, the other party may cancel this lease and claim damages.

10. SPECIAL CONDITIONS

10.1 The Premises shall be used for residential purposes only.

10.2 No pets may be kept without the Landlord's prior written consent.

10.3 The Tenant may not sublet the Premises or cede this lease without the Landlord's prior written consent.

11. PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION

11.1 Each party consents to the other processing the personal information in this lease solely for the purpose of administering the lease, in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013.

12. GENERAL

12.1 This document is the whole agreement. No variation is binding unless reduced to writing and signed by both parties.
